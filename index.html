Software Engineer
to Content Creator
Journey.

My journey from software engineering to content creation grew naturally from two worlds I have always been part of: technology and education. As a technology professional and former teacher, I learned how to solve complex problems and, more importantly, how to explain them.

Growing up in Nepal, I also saw firsthand how corruption, mismanagement, and lack of awareness can shape people's lives. Today, I bring these experiences together through evidence-based videos designed to inform, educate, and encourage people to look beyond headlines and understand the facts.
